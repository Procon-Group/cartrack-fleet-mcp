Cost per kilometre
Fleet-card Rand ÷ Cartrack-metered km, by vehicle
How this is calculated: km comes from Cartrack trip distances for the target month (odometer-delta history isn't accumulated far back enough yet — this will switch over automatically once enough daily readings exist). Flags trigger at a 15% deviation, checked two ways: against that vehicle's own trailing average, and against the average for its division. A vehicle can be flagged on one, both, or neither.
Fuel efficiency
Fuel-card litres ÷ Cartrack-metered km, by vehicle
How this is calculated: litres per 100km, using the same target month and Cartrack trip-distance km as Cost/KM. Flags trigger at a 15% deviation from either that vehicle's own trailing average or its division average — a spike can mean fuel-card misuse, a leak, or heavy idling; a drop can mean under-recorded fuel purchases.
Flagged vehicles
Fuel threshold, speeding and harsh-driving exceptions in one view
How flags are set: Fuel — this month's fuel-card litres exceed the vehicle's monthly limit. Speeding — at least one road-limit or fleet-threshold speeding event, last 30 days. Harsh driving — at least one harsh braking, cornering or acceleration event, last 30 days. Sorted by total flags, most first.
Not proof of anything on its own. A second fill-up on the same card, same day, can be a genuine second trip, a re-swipe after a declined card, or a slip that got captured twice — but it is also the pattern card misuse leaves behind, so it is worth a quick look. Pay closer attention to pairs at different stations, pairs with no litres captured (rand-only swipes), or slip numbers that do not match the vehicle's usual supplier.

By driver
Only drivers with at least one speeding event
Two speeding measures: road-limit events are speed over the actual road's posted limit; fleet-threshold events are speed over a flat threshold configured in Fleetweb (if configured — may read zero if that feature isn't set up on this account). Both are shown since they catch different things.
Not Cartrack's official score. Cartrack's Fleet API has no driver-scoring or ranking endpoint at all (confirmed against the live API spec — only /drivers, /delivery/drivers, and a beta /coaching/events with no score/rank fields), so the "Driver Scores" view in Fleetweb's own portal isn't reachable here. This is Procon's own estimate instead: each driver's harsh-driving rate, speeding rate and idle share, last 30 days, ranked against this fleet's own drivers — 100 is best-in-fleet, 0 is worst-in-fleet, not an absolute benchmark. Drivers with fewer than 3 trips in the window aren't scored.
